Why does AI hallucinate? Why does ChatGPT make things up, and say it like it's 100% sure? It's not lying. It's bluffing, like a student guessing in an exam.

In about 4 minutes: what an AI hallucination really is, what OpenAI's own researchers found about why it happens, two real cases (a lawyer's fake court cases and an airline chatbot), and three simple habits to fact-check AI answers.
